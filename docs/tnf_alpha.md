# TNF-alpha workflow and lessons

TNF-alpha was treated as a soluble homotrimer. Receptor-binding surfaces span adjacent protomers, so generation and screening must retain the neighboring-protomer environment and terminal context.

## Geometry and symmetry

Normalize chain labels and residue mappings before comparing structures. Enumerate target protomer correspondences, perform proper-rotation target alignment, and examine the three C3-related mappings of the target-only best correspondence. Report the mapping and target fit as well as antibody displacement. A label-permuted equivalent pose must not be mistaken for a new binding mode. Conversely, symmetry must not excuse a deformed trimer or severe clash.

Changing H3 alone, broader CDR remodeling, approach rotation and light-chain engagement were treated as distinct hypotheses. Physical screening and sequence-to-pose recovery were tracked separately. An apparently well-packed conditioned model may still fail independent folding.

## Cross-species and conditional binding

The intended conditional direction was human TNF binding at pH 7.4 and weaker binding at pH 6.0. Mouse recognition was evaluated with the same H/L sequence at neutral pH. Assess homologous footprint and contact-pair retention, including conserved charged partners.

Site searches considered burial, solvent accessibility, contact frequency, local polar/basic/acidic chemistry and explicit His rotamers. The pH hypotheses included buried protonated-His penalties and repulsion near basic target residues. Nearby acidic partners can reverse the intended direction. Multiple His substitutions do not establish cooperative switching.

PROPKA bound/free estimates and rigid proton-linkage calculations were used with matched neutral controls. Their output is a model contribution, not total binding free energy or quantitative selectivity. Some final decisions used the explicitly disclosed design-intent standard with locally conditioned human/mouse compatibility; that is weaker than independent sequence-only recovery. No designed sequence, substitution panel or selected structure is supplied in this repository.
